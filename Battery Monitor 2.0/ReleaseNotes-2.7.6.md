**Release: Battery Monitor 2.0 v2.7.6**
Choose your date format and clock.

**Problem:**
- Dates were always shown as MM/DD/YYYY, which reads backwards in the UK and much of the world, and times mixed 12 and 24-hour formats.

**The Fix:**
- New **Dates and times** section on the Scan interval & dates page:
  - **Date format:** MM/DD/YYYY (US), DD/MM/YYYY (UK, EU) or YYYY-MM-DD.
  - **Clock:** 12-hour or 24-hour.
- Applies to the replacement history, notifications, the snooze time and the web portal.
- Display only: your existing history is kept as is and shows in the new format right away.
- Nothing changes until you pick a format. The Battery Summary notification now follows the clock setting too (12-hour unless you choose 24-hour).

**Action needed:**
Update through HPM. To change the format, open **Scan interval & dates**.
